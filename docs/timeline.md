## 2025

On 6/10/2025, approximately 1 year before the Board of Aldermen's work session where this project was first officially presented to the board, the business "Starkville Energy LLC" was registered with the Mississippi Secretary of State's Office.   
The Certificate of Formation can be found at [this link](https://corp.sos.ms.gov/corpconv/portal/c/ExecuteWorkflow.aspx?workflowid=g12dbd558-fa5d-49a1-a869-ad8b9db198db&FilingId=6fbeb8f3-3a43-41fa-9e3e-891572306794).  

The business email, business "organizer", and registered agent are all the same entity: Registered Agents Inc.  
This is a national business based in Wyoming; see this [Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Registered_Agents_Inc.) for more information.  
See this [Annual Report for Essential Preservation Solutions LLC](https://wyobiz.wyo.gov/Business/DownloadPDF.aspx?ID=238180030020232047026077171052120030054049216255&TYPE=AR) from the Wyoming Secretary of State to see the name "Robin Jones" listed as the contact.   
This is the same name as the "Organizer" on the Certificate of Formation for Starkville Energy LLC linked above.   
According to a person I contacted at Registered Agents Inc, Robin Jones is just the person who signs these documents and has no actual business interest (i.e. ownership) of these companies. 

I also want to note that the "NAICS Code/Nature of Business" section is given as "Lessors of Nonresidential Buildings" on this listing as well as several other listings that may be involved with the facility (more on that later).   
There is an entry for "Virtual currency (cryptocurrency) mining" in the 2022 NAICS ([518210](https://www.census.gov/naics/?input=crypto&year=2022&details=518210)), but since I'm not a business law person, I can't tell you if their choice here is important.	



## July 2026

### Prior to 6/12/2026 work session
(As far as I can tell,) Mayor Spruill or someone else in the city was contacted by TVA (Tennessee Valley Authority, who Starkville Utilities buys power from) about this proposed project, and a presentation was organized for the Board of Aldermen’s 6/12 work session.

In addition, one Sunday (not sure which, but we could ask), Mayor Spruill was accompanied by Alderwoman Sistrunk on a trip to Puryear, TN, where they took a decibel reading using a cell phone app near a facility supposedly owned by the same developer/company.

### After 6/12/2026 Work Session
On 6/16/2026, the Dispatch published an article titled ["Crypto mining center pitched for Industrial Park Road"](https://cdispatch.com/news/crypto-mining-center-pitched-for-industrial-park-road/) covering the board's work session.

Over the next couple of weeks, this article was shared to various places on social media (e.g., Facebook, Nextdoor, and others; I became aware of it thanks to a user in the Tech Connect GTR Discord server). 
Discussion began and many citizens became worried about some of the aspects of the project, especially when it was positioned as a "data center" at a time when there is a lot of public scrutiny towards data centers.
For example, the [xAI facilities in Memphis](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/class-action-lawsuit-filed-over-xai-complaints-in-southaven/ar-AA25j6sJ) and [proposed facility in Southaven](https://mississippi.org/news/tech-leader-xai-investing-more-than-20-billion-in-southaven/) have generated mixed sentiment, with local and state officials being mostly enthusiastic and community members being more negative. 

On 6/17/2026, Alderman Pochop posted on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1Btj5pd8KT/) about an upcoming town hall event he planned to host.

### 7/14/2026 - Alderman Pochop's Town Hall
Turnout was pretty high for Alderman Pochop's aforementioned town hall. 
Very few empty seats in the room. 
I believe most attendees were either from Ward 5 (Pochop's ward) or were there to talk about the cryptomining facility. The main scheduled topic was an informative presentation about road maintenance and improvements, both currently underway and upcoming. 

However, the bulk of the discussions and questions in the Q&A portion regarded the cryptomining facility; Mayor Spruill was in attendance and answered some questions. Many of us stayed behind afterwards to discuss the facility and gather contact information.

## Between Meetings
During downtime, citizens that had exchanged contact information worked to find out more about the project. 

Important findings are as follows:
- Per Mayor Spruill, the company listed on the land parcel is [Waring Investments, Inc.](https://opencorporates.com/companies/us_ms/584853); note this company also uses NAIC code "Lessors of Nonresidential Buildings."
- Per public documents, the Puryear, TN facility is owned by [TN Wall Street LLC](https://opencorporates.com/companies/us_tn/001621831) and/or [Puryear Electric LLC](https://opencorporates.com/companies/us_tn/001561999). (This parent company seems to like naming their LLCs after the city they're building in.)
- Per public documents, the Puryear facility is subleased by TN Wall Street LLC and/or Puryear Electric LLC to [Old Const LLC](https://opencorporates.com/companies/us_tn/001597284), "which is charged with finding space/secure electricity around the country for clients that want to run their mining computers."
- Per public documents, Old Const LLC's client in Puryear is/was [Bitmain Technologies Georgia Limited](https://opencorporates.com/companies/us_tn/001545872)
- Per the names of their directors/officers, it is possible that at least either Old Const LLC or Bitmain are Chinese companies with a [U.S. domestic entity](https://www.sillscummis.com/considerations-for-chinese-companies-doing-business-in-the-united-states/)

To summarize (since these nesting doll companies get confusing), the developer that wants to build the cryptomining facility in Starkville owns another mine in Puryear, TN. That mine seems to be leased to another company (Old Const LLC), who then seems to have leased to another company (Bitmain).
