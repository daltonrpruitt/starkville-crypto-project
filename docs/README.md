## Basic Chronology

I will try to give a simple overview of relevant events, with details added in later.
This is all from my perspective, at least so far. 
Others may add input later.

### 2025

On 6/10/2025, approximately 1 year before the Board of Aldermen's work session where this project was first officially presented to the board, the business "Starkville Energy LLC" was registered with the Mississippi Secretary of State's Office.   
The Certificate of Formation can be found at [this link](https://corp.sos.ms.gov/corpconv/portal/c/ExecuteWorkflow.aspx?workflowid=g12dbd558-fa5d-49a1-a869-ad8b9db198db&FilingId=6fbeb8f3-3a43-41fa-9e3e-891572306794).  
You can see further that the business email, business "organizer", and registered agent are all the same entity: Registered Agents Inc.  
This is a national business based in Wyoming; see this [Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Registered_Agents_Inc.) for more information.  
See this [Annual Report for Essential Preservation Solutions LLC](https://wyobiz.wyo.gov/Business/DownloadPDF.aspx?ID=238180030020232047026077171052120030054049216255&TYPE=AR) from the Wyoming Secretary of State to see the name "Robin Jones" listed as the contact.   
This is the same name as the "Organizer" on the Certificate of Formation for Starkville Energy LLC linked above.   
According to a person I contacted at Registered Agents Inc, Robin Jones is just the person who signs these documents and has no actual business interest (i.e. ownership) of these companies. 

I also want to note that the "NAICS Code/Nature of Business" section is given as "Lessors of Nonresidential Buildings".   
While I *can* say that there is an entry for "Virtual currency (cryptocurrency) mining" in the 2022 NAICS ([518210](https://www.census.gov/naics/?input=crypto&year=2022&details=518210)), I'm not a business law person, so I can't tell you if their choice here is important.

#### Prior to 6/12/2026 work session
Mayor Spruill (or someone in the city) is contacted by TVA (Tennessee Valley Authority) (as far as I can tell) about this proposed project and sets up everything involved in setting up a presentation for the Board of Aldermen's 6/12 work session.

In addition, Mayor Spruill requests Alderwoman Sistrunk accompany her on a trip to the Puryear location on a Sunday (not sure which, but we could ask), where they took a decibel reading.

#### 6/12/2026 - Board of Aldermen Work Session
The Board of Aldermen had their work session, and three presenters discussed the project with the board: an Executive Directory of the Tennessee Municipal Electric Power Association, a TVA Customer Service Manager (as far as I can tell), and the President of Atwell and Gent (a local engineering/consulting firm).
Several questions were asked and answered. 
See my notes linked above or the full meeting recording for more details.

### After 6/12/2026 Work Session
On 6/16/2026, the Dispatch published an article titled "Crypto mining center pitched for Industrial Park Road" covering the board's work session. 

Over the next couple of weeks, this article was shared to various places on social media (e.g., Facebook, Nextdoor, and others; I became aware of it thanks to a user in the Tech Connect GTR Discord server). 
Discussion began and many citizens became worried about some of the aspects of the project, especially when it was positioned as a "data center" at a time when data centers are primarily associated with "artificial intelligence". 
For example, the [xAI facilities in Memphis](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/class-action-lawsuit-filed-over-xai-complaints-in-southaven/ar-AA25j6sJ) and [proposed facility in Southaven](https://mississippi.org/news/tech-leader-xai-investing-more-than-20-billion-in-southaven/) have generated mixed sentiment, with local and state officials being mostly enthusiastic and community members being more negative. 

On 6/17/2026, Alderman Pochop posted on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1Btj5pd8KT/) about an upcoming town hall event he planned to host, but I'm not sure when it was originally planned and announced. 
This is the earliest mention I could find without scouring Facebook for hours...


### 7/7/2026 - Board of Aldermen Regular Meeting
According to the published agenda ([link](https://starkvillems.portal.civicclerk.com/event/198/files/agenda/2980)), someone was scheduled to speak about the "cryptomining center", but during the meeting the mayor mentioned that the public appearance had been pulled from the agenda at the request of the presenter prior to the meeting ([link to part of recording where mayor said this](https://www.youtube.com/live/f9ZzUbbYvHY?si=Okn3Dsw9D0DymlmB&t=131)).

Several citizens made comments in regard to the cryptomining facility. 
After the citizens' comments section, the mayor stated that the answers to several of the issues raised by the citizens' comments were addressed in the 6/12/2026 [work session](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kcb5lYlrLu8) that is available online. (Writer's note: I am one of the citizens that commented, and the mayor is 100% correct in that at least some of those issues were addressed in that work session.)

Several of us talked afterwards, and a few of us gathered names and email addresses for further communication.
