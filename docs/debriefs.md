## Debriefs on Organzational Efforts

A few of us worked to distill the organizing experiences we had in this effort
in an attempt to help other potential organizers with their own efforts, 
now and in the future.

This is much more *descriptive* than it is *prescriptive*, 
and we lay out specifically that things that seemed to work for our 
community in this specific case may have workd out for reasons 
outside of our control. 
Even so, what we learned may help, and we want to give others a chance 
to learn from our experience if possible.

These two documents represent the perspectives of two separate individuals:
myself (Dalton) and one of my fellow organizers (EB). 

The first document is a bit over 4 pages and is by EB. 
Part of their goal was to make a document easier to read than the one that I wrote,
but it is still from their own perspective.

if you are deeply interested in plugging into similar community efforts 
in the future. 
Even just recognizing when other people are trying to do some of the 
things we discuss may help you know when to jump forth and help!
