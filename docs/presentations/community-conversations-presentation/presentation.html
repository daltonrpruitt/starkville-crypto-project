Guidelines for Speeches/Speaking

  • Writing
  • Delivery
  • Flexibility

Writing

  • Write out your thoughts ahead of time
    • Maybe get feedback from peers?
  • Share why you care and what you want
  • Grounded in facts, not opinions
  • Don’t tell jokes
  • Be respectful; don’t be accusatory
  • Your audience is the officials in front of you, not the community behind you
  • Be brief (ideally <2-3 minutes, especially if speaking on behalf of yourself)

Delivery

  • Dress presentably
  • Calm and measured, not overly emotional or “hysterical”
  • Practice a few times; time yourself
  • I don’t think we have time to pass out any visual aids (or use the presentation media)
  • Breathe

Flexibility

  • Realize that we may not all get to speak, depending on what rules are enforced
  • The numbers and overall sentiment may mean more than the specific arguments made, but we should still try to be coherent and respectful
